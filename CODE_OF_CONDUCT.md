# Code of Conduct

CCD_HACKBOX welcomes contributors and learners.

All participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect.
- Communicate professionally.
- Respect different experience levels.
- Share knowledge responsibly.
- Avoid harassment and discrimination.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Follow ethical cybersecurity practices.

Project maintainers may moderate contributions and community participation
that violate these expectations.
